# 🚀 SEO Rank Tracer & SERP Analyzer Chrome Extension

A lightweight, powerful Chrome extension designed for real-time SEO position tracking and competitor insights. Instead of switching to heavy third-party dashboards, monitor your keyword health and optimize your pages directly alongside live Google search results.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🎯 **Set & Forget Dashboard**: Save your target domain and easily add the core keywords you want to monitor.
* 📈 **Live Trend Tracking**: Every time you search, see your domain's exact rank today compared to its performance over the **last 7 days** and **15 days**.
* 🕵️‍♂️ **Organic Competitor Intel**: View the top organic competition for your tracked keywords at a glance without leaving the SERP.
* 🔍 **On-The-Fly Gap Analysis**: Instantly compare your domain's Meta Title and Meta Description against top-ranking pages to identify exactly which missing keywords are costing you rankings.
* 💻 **Sidebar Integration**: The entire tool sits inside your browser, sliding in beautifully next to your active search page.

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. **Configure Your Project**: Enter your website's domain and input your target keyword list.
2. **Search Normally**: Browse Google. The extension automatically highlights your domain and tracks its rank history.
3. **Analyze the Gap**: Click the gap analysis button on any search page to see what top-performing competitors are doing better.
